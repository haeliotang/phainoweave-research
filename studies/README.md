# Research studies

This directory indexes PhainoWeave's public research series. Each series is an independent empirical unit with its own proposition, measurement model, reference, preregistration and evidence status.

The shared program is [Empirical Limits of Physical-World Observability](../docs/research/observability-program.md). Shared vocabulary does not imply shared evidence or a shared result. A series may be proposed, at G0/G1, or published; these states are not interchangeable.

| **PW-H Hydrology** | Proposed; no public preregistration or result | [hydrology/README.md](hydrology/README.md) |
| **PW-S Satellite** | Proposed; no public preregistration or result | [satellite/README.md](satellite/README.md) |
| **PW-R RF** | Proposed; no authorised public experiment | [rf/README.md](rf/README.md) |
| **PW-V Visual** | Proposed; no authorised public camera study | [visual/README.md](visual/README.md) |

## Status vocabulary

- **Proposed** — a research question and plan exist; no public preregistration, data audit or result.
- **Preregistered** — a public, frozen protocol exists; no result is implied.
- **Evaluated** — a reproducible result package exists at the declared evidence level.
- **Falsified** — a preregistered claim failed; the negative result is retained.

The acoustic baseline is a published synthetic series under tag `research-2026-11-07`. It is not evidence for the proposed hydrology, satellite, RF or visual series.
