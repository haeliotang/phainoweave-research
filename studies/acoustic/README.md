# PW-A — Acoustic series

**Status:** published synthetic baseline; E1-0 prepared / not evaluated
**Snapshot:** tag [`research-2026-11-07`](https://github.com/haeliotang/phainoweave-research/tree/research-2026-11-07)
**Framework:** [Empirical Limits of Physical-World Observability](../../docs/research/observability-program.md)

This is the first published PhainoWeave research series. It asks whether physically constrained acoustic observations can support or contradict declared non-person motion propositions, and when the observations are insufficient.

It is one series, not the whole program.

## Published evidence

- [E0 synthetic Doppler](../../docs/experiments/2026-11-04-e0-synthetic-doppler.md) — 2,500 preregistered synthetic trials, replayable digest, measured tolerances and failure thresholds. Synthetic only.
- [C1 identifiability contract](../../docs/experiments/2026-11-05-c1-identifiability.md) — symbolic proof, independent certificate and locked claim validation for one acoustic forward model.
- [C2-C sealed confirmation](../../docs/experiments/2026-11-05-c2-sealed-confirmation.md) — one 566-trial sealed run; B1 conditionally supported on the declared endpoint, with the published high-chirp family failure and unresolved time-block caveat.
- [E1-C estimator audit](../../docs/experiments/2026-11-05-e1-estimator-audit.md), [trial-level decision stability](../../docs/experiments/2026-11-05-c2-sealed-confirmation.md), and the R1-D/R2 cross-environment reports retain negative and unresolved results.

Frozen protocols are under [`protocols/`](../../protocols/). The sealed archive and independent recomputation path are under [`evidence/c2c-sealed/`](../../evidence/c2c-sealed/). The public snapshot tag must not be moved.

## What is not established

- No real bench measurement exists; E1 remains `prepared / not_evaluated`.
- No real-world localization, tracking or physical capability is claimed.
- Cross-platform byte identity (R2) is not established; R1-D is not a blocking gate.
- The acoustic series does not establish any claim for PW-H, PW-S, PW-R or PW-V.

## Next acoustic gate

E1-0 requires lawful real recordings, independent frequency/time references and calibration. Until that evidence exists, this series remains at its synthetic baseline and the prepared E1-0 state.
