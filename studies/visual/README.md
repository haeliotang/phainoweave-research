# PW-V — Visual series

**Status:** proposed; no authorised public camera study
**Framework:** [Empirical Limits of Physical-World Observability](../../docs/research/observability-program.md)

## Proposition family

For a lawfully usable fixed camera and a declared view, did a specified environmental boundary — for example a river water line at a fixed structure — cross a preregistered image-space or physical threshold during the declared window?

The proposition concerns an environmental boundary, not a person, vehicle, residence or activity.

## Measurement mechanisms under study

- fixed-view imagery with known installation geometry and timestamp quality;
- explicit projection, illumination, occlusion and camera-calibration model;
- no end-to-end semantic model whose features cannot be inspected in the first feasibility study.

## Independent reference

A physical boundary measurement — surveyed water line, independent stage/gauge or other non-image observation — that is not derived from the same image. A visible numeric gauge readout is label leakage and is excluded or masked before analysis.

## Current status

Proposed. No camera feed, site, public preregistration or result is authorised or claimed. Public availability of a camera stream is not permission for collection, training or redistribution.

## Public outputs expected at each gate

- G0: camera licence, access, geometry, timestamp and independent-reference audit;
- G1: projection/illumination/occlusion model;
- G2: distinguishable and undetermined boundary propositions with counterexamples;
- G3/G4: real-data and locked-validation packages, if lawful data exist.

No result exists yet.
