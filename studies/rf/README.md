# PW-R — RF series

**Status:** proposed; no authorised public experiment
**Framework:** [Empirical Limits of Physical-World Observability](../../docs/research/observability-program.md)

## Proposition family

In a controlled, authorised environment, can a declared non-person reflector, barrier or medium state be distinguished from alternative physical configurations using measured channel-state information under a stated propagation model?

This is a physical-environment question. It excludes people, activity, identity, occupancy and personal devices.

## Measurement mechanisms under study

- controlled CSI/transfer measurements with known transmitter, receiver, orientation and calibration;
- explicit multipath/propagation model with unknown gains, delays and hardware response;
- IEEE 802.11bf-2025 as relevant standards context, not as an authorisation or a validated model.

## Independent reference

An independent measurement of the physical target state — position, material, orientation or controlled medium — not derived from the CSI stream.

## Current status

Proposed. No equipment, dataset, public preregistration or result is authorised or claimed. Public CSI datasets with human-activity labels are not treated as generic environmental data.

## Public outputs expected at each gate

- G0: authorisation, spectrum, hardware, privacy and reference audit;
- G1: multipath model and calibration/nuisance bounds;
- G2: indistinguishable target-state counterexamples;
- G3/G4: controlled real-data and locked-validation packages, if authorised.

No result exists yet.
