# PR-4d plan — Model Adequacy & Scientific Decision Utility

**Status:** in progress · 2026-11-05 · **Authority:** [ADR 0015](../decisions/0015-model-adequacy-and-epistemic-decisions.md) · **Prior:** [PR-4c validation](2026-11-05-pr4c-hardened-validation.md)

**Goal.** Not "replace variance-explained with a whiteness test", but a Model Adequacy & Discrepancy layer that distinguishes **model-form error**, **insufficient observation information**, and **wrong parameter acceptance** — and an independent evaluation that shows whether the system's scientific decisions become more reliable at the same retention.

**Priority.** Higher than closing the last 2499/2500 cross-platform classification difference and higher than any new data adapter. E1 field data collection remains gated on venue/consent/calibration/legal review.

## The 600 false accepts, by mechanism (diagnostic baseline)

From the PR-4c hardened artifact against synthetic truth, 2000 accepted / 600 outside the frozen parameter tolerances:

| Mechanism | Conditions | Accepted | Wrong | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model misspecification — acceleration | `accel_0.25`, `accel_0.5` | 200 | 200 (100%) | unmodelled dynamics; variance-explained accepts a biased constant-velocity fit |
| Model misspecification — clock rate | `clock_20000ppm`, `clock_50000ppm` | 200 | 200 (100%) | nominal clock cannot represent a 2–5% rate error |
| Observational equivalence — clock offset | `offset_0.3s`, `offset_2s` | 200 | 200 (100%) | `β` is exactly degenerate with `t0`; no residual test can recover physical event time without an external reference |
| Within tolerance | `accel_0.1`, clock ≤1000 ppm, `window_20s`, geometry 100/60 m, time noise, amplitude | 1400 | 0 | correct |
| Rejected by the gate | `window_5s/10s`, geometry 30 km, null | 0 accepted | — | 225 valid trials lost to indeterminate in the hardened rule |

The three wrong-accept families require different responses: adequacy tests can detect (1) and (2) if the omitted structure leaves a signature; (3) needs a degeneracy declaration and, eventually, an independent time reference — not a better residual test.

## Data layers and split design

| Layer | Contents | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Original E0 | the frozen artifact and report | historical/diagnostic only; never used as validation |
| Tuning | E0 conditions used to calibrate the adequacy diagnostics and thresholds: `accel_0.1`, `clock_1000ppm`, `offset_0.3s`, `snr_+5dB`, `window_20s`, `geom_v100_b300`, `amp_*`, `null` | thresholds, lag order and null distributions calibrated here; every choice logged |
| Locked Validation | **new seeds** and **unseen perturbation levels**: acceleration {0.175, 0.35, 0.75} m/s², clock rate {5 000, 35 000} ppm, clock offset {0.15, 1.0, 5.0} s, windows {12, 30} s, closest approach {200, 800, 10 000} m, new noise levels; same generator, unseen parameter combinations | frozen before opening; no adaptation afterwards; stratified by mechanism |

The locked grid is generated by the experiment module, not by resampling E0 trials: a random trial split would let the method memorise E0's failure patterns.

## Metrics (no fixed target)

- **Risk–coverage curve** for the old rule and the new adequacy layer: false-accept rate among accepted vs retention (fraction of valid trials accepted), per mechanism.
- **Matched-retention comparison**: at each of the new rule's operating retentions, the old rule's false-accept rate on the same condition strata.
- **Declared-but-unmet boundary results**: for mechanisms where the observation is information-theoretically insufficient (clock offset ↔ `t0`), the result is the declared boundary plus the independent measurement needed, not an improved false-accept number.
- Intervals clustered by condition/series; per-mechanism counts always shown; no pooled number alone.

## Acceptance

1. The new layer distinguishes at least the three failure families on the locked layer, with calibration and degeneracy evidence, and without using truth in the decision.
2. On the locked layer, at matched valid-retention, the new rule's false-accept rate is lower than the old rule's for the mechanisms it claims to detect — or the report shows which mechanisms remain undetectable and why.
3. The epistemic decision `undetermined` is produced for observational-equivalence cases instead of a wrong `supported`.
4. Cross-environment validation (A/B/C) shows identical epistemic decisions on the locked layer, or the differences are classified by the ADR 0013 taxonomy.
5. Negative results are published: a family the layer cannot detect is a result, not a reason to widen a threshold.

## Separation of duties

The adequacy method is implemented by one workstream; the evaluation harness (artifact-only, no method imports) by another. Neither reviews its own numbers. The report is assembled by the Lead with both artifacts and the frozen protocol digest.
