**Status:** template, not legal advice · 2026-11-05 · **Applies to:** E1 Pilot recordings · **Governing policy:** [ethics](../policy/ethics-and-privacy.md), [data governance](data-governance.md)

This template is prepared in advance of the pilot. ## 2. What is collected

- Audio recordings (uncompressed WAV) of a controlled moving sound source.
- Video recordings used as independent geometric truth.
- Motion and timing notes; optionally phone GNSS/IMU traces.
- Environment: temperature, humidity, wind, surface notes.
- No personal profile data, no biometric identification, no voice-content analysis.

## 3. Purpose and limits

- Purpose: test whether a physical Doppler model holds on real observations; identify model misfit, parameter degeneracy and undecidable states.
- Not used for: geo-location of persons, surveillance, targeting, enforcement, commercial profiling.
- Results published only as desensitized features, parameters, hashes and reproducible non-sensitive outputs; raw media and precise locations are not published in the pilot report.
