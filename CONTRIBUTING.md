# Contributing to PhainoWeave Research

PhainoWeave is a research program. Useful contributions are counterexamples,
checkable specifications, licensed data paths and small verified
implementations.

## Evidence before claims

Claims must point to a test, a run record or a published report. Unverified
numbers stay marked `unverified`.

## Contribution licensing

All contributions require the [Inceptless CLA](CLA.md). Sign it at
<https://cla-assistant.io/haeliotang/phainoweave-research>. A PR is merged only
after the CLA check (`license/cla`) passes.


## Pull requests

- Keep one area per pull request.
- State the commands and results you ran.
- Keep negative results and their artifacts.
